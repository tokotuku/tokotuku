## Props

| Prop | Type | Required | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | `number` | Yes | Product route and cart identifier |
| `name` | `string` | Yes | Product heading and accessible link label |
| `description` | `string` | Yes | Two-line summary |
| `price` | `string` | Yes | Already formatted display price |
| `priceCents` | `number` | Yes | Exact cart price in minor currency units |
| `imageUrl` | `string` | Yes | Product media URL |

The add button serializes `id`, `name`, `priceCents`, and `imageUrl` into `data-product`. `SiteHeader` listens for the click and persists it to the local cart.
