## What Karsa provides

- A typed Astro UI and theme layer with accessible primitives.
- Optional modules with registry contributions, dependency order, migrations, and seeds.
- Content and page patterns that keep app-owned pages separate from module-owned routes.
- An AI workflow that records assumptions, evidence, validation, and scope decisions.

Use the guides to choose a preset, then follow the matching contract for your site's content, data, and deployment.
