title: Publication site guide

The publication preset is content-first. Treat articles, authors, tags, feeds, and release dates as editorial data, not as a disguised product catalog.

## Content ownership

Keep source entries in the content layer and keep the application responsible for the shell, navigation, landing pages, and reading layout. Use the content/blog authoring guide for frontmatter and editorial checks. A publication does not need catalog, order, or booking modules unless a separate brief explicitly introduces that domain.

## Tiers

- Install: validate the content loader, routes, theme, and registry.
- Content: add approved articles, authors, tags, descriptions, and images.
- Polished: tune typography, reading measure, and editorial imagery without creating a CMS abstraction prematurely.

Check canonical URLs, headings, alternative text, dates in the configured locale, feed output, draft handling, broken links, typecheck, lint, and build. Report missing editorial input instead of inventing it.
