Choose the site shape first, then choose the maturity tier. The tier is permission, not a guess about how much work the brief might eventually need.

| Preset | Use it for | Ownership default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company | A profile, marketing site, or organization presence | App-owned Astro pages and sections |
| Product | A site that presents purchasable items | Product and order modules own domain routes; the app owns brand pages |
| Service | A site that presents bookable work | Booking adapters own scheduling; the app owns explanatory pages |
| Publication | Articles, notes, documentation, or a newsroom | Content owns entries and feeds; the app owns shell and editorial landing pages |

| Tier | What it permits | Stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Install | Packages, module wiring, and migration/registry checks | Do not add content or visual polish |
| Content | Install plus copy, data, and page content | Keep packaged layout and tokens |
| Polished | Content plus deliberate theme and local assets | Still respect module ownership; no speculative systems |

Never raise a tier without the requester's permission. If a brief is ambiguous, ask or state the lowest reasonable tier and list what remains out of scope.

## Selection checklist

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `astro.config.mjs`, `package.json`, and the resolved registry.
2. Identify the preset from the requested user journey, not from a preferred package.
3. Record the authorized tier and its stop condition.
4. List pages, modules, seed data, and assets with their owner.
5. Define validation evidence before implementation.

The selected preset and tier belong in the plan and the final report so another agent can continue without guessing.
