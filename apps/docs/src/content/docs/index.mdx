## Available components

- [`tk-button`](/components/button/) — actions with three variants and sizes.
- [`tk-input`](/components/input/) — labeled text, email, and password fields.
- [`tk-card`](/components/card/) — a composable media, body, and footer container.

## Starter component library

The commerce starter also includes 18 application-level Astro components for dashboards and storefronts. Browse them under **Starter components** in the sidebar, beginning with [`AppButton`](/starter/core/app-button/).
