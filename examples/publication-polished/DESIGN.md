# Karsa Journal design manifest

## Palette

Karsa Journal uses quiet editorial warmth at a human scale:

- Light accent: `#b85c38` (burnt clay)
- Light accent foreground: `#fffaf2` (paper white)
- Dark accent: `#f0a276` (apricot highlight)
- Dark accent foreground: `#291914` (brown-black)
- Surfaces: warm paper and soft cream panels

Typography pairs expressive serif headlines with a calm, legible sans-serif body. The page keeps
the content module's blog, preview, publish, archive, RSS, and sitemap ownership intact.
