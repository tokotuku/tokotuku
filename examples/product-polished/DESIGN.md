# Racik Rasa design contract

## Palette

Warm Indonesian pantry editorial: grounded burnt clay and toasted coffee in light mode,
with a dark mode that keeps the same spice-and-tea character without lowering contrast.

| Token | Light | Dark | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accent | `#9A4427` | `#E3A16E` | Primary actions, links, active states |
| Accent foreground | `#FFF9F0` | `#2A1710` | Text/icons on accent |
| Supporting ink | `#2A211D` | `#F8ECDD` | Headlines and body emphasis |
| Supporting surface | `#F5E8D7` | `#3B2820` | Cards, quiet sections, ingredient warmth |
| Supporting green | `#4E6647` | `#9BB58D` | Freshness and stock/success cues |

Brand identity: Racik Rasa. Locale: `id-ID`. Currency: `IDR`. Time zone: `Asia/Jakarta`.

## Exact raster asset manifest

Exactly five distinct generated raster assets are required. Each is generated by one built-in
image-generation call, inspected after generation, and saved locally. No other raster asset is
part of this fixture.

| ID | Purpose | Output path | Media key / config reference | Dimensions | Format |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `hero` | Storefront hero: Indonesian pantry ingredients and warm table scene | `public/images/racik-rasa-hero.png` | `/images/racik-rasa-hero.png` | 1536 × 1024 | PNG |
| `kopi` | Product image shared by the two coffee products | `seed/media/products/racik-rasa-kopi.png` | `products/racik-rasa-kopi.png` | 1254 × 1254 | PNG |
| `rempah` | Product image shared by the two spice products | `seed/media/products/racik-rasa-rempah.png` | `products/racik-rasa-rempah.png` | 1254 × 1254 | PNG |
| `teh` | Product image for the tea product | `seed/media/products/racik-rasa-teh.png` | `products/racik-rasa-teh.png` | 1254 × 1254 | PNG |
| `sambal` | Product image for the sambal product | `seed/media/products/racik-rasa-sambal.png` | `products/racik-rasa-sambal.png` | 1254 × 1254 | PNG |

Constraints for every asset: locally stored, editorial product photography, no stock/external
URLs, no placeholders, no logo, no legible text, no watermark, no data URI, and no API/CLI
generation fallback.
