# Arunika Energi design manifest

## Palette

Arunika Energi uses a calm, credible energy-infrastructure palette with Indonesian
editorial warmth:

- Light accent: `#d88a2c` (sunlit amber)
- Light accent foreground: `#102b3a` (deep forest blue-black)
- Dark accent: `#f2b45d` (amber highlight)
- Dark accent foreground: `#102b3a`
- Surfaces: warm ivory and pale mist-blue panels
- Support: muted sage for operational and sustainability notes
- Dark surface: midnight blue-black with amber highlights

Typography pairs strong, compact display headlines with quiet, highly legible
sans-serif body copy.
